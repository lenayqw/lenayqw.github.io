Lena Wang

I am a senior at Johns Hopkins University studying Computer Science and Molecular & Cellular Biology, where I am fortunate to be mentored by Prof. Alexis Battle.

I am generally interested in building deep learning models that can understand and predict how genetic variants contribute to diseases. My recent work studies how large-scale personalized genomic data can be better utilized to improve sequence-to-function models.

Before working on computational genomics, I worked with Prof. Xiaobo Mao on understanding the mechanisms behind neurodegenerative diseases.
